❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is Alurtra?
Alurtra is a free phishing and scam detector built for Canadians. It checks URLs and emails against known threat databases and AI analysis to flag suspicious links before you click them.
How does the phishing detector work?
Alurtra analyzes a URL's structure (domain age, subdomain patterns, HTTPS usage, and more), cross-references it against Google Safe Browsing and URLhaus malware databases, and uses AI to assess context. Each check returns a risk score from 0–100 with a LOW, MEDIUM, or HIGH rating.
Can Alurtra check emails too?
Yes. Alurtra scans email content for urgency language, financial fraud indicators, personal information harvesting attempts, and Canadian-specific scam patterns involving organizations like the CRA, OSAP, and major banks.
Is Alurtra free to use?
Yes, Alurtra is completely free. You can check URLs and emails without an account, and sign up for free to save your scan history.
